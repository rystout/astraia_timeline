How to read this page
Fixed
Stated precisely in the source files.
Approx
The sources give it as approximate (“~”).
Estimated
A “years ago” figure or a range, so the exact year is uncertain.
Derived
Computed from another reckoning — for example the retired F.Y. count.
A.S.
After Sundering: year 0 is the Sundering; negative years are written P.A. (pre-Azyrra).
A.K.
Anno Kaironis: 1 A.K. = 8,725 A.S. Negative A.K. years are written B.K. (before 1 A.K.).
ybp
Years before present, measured back from the present year on the calibration panel.
